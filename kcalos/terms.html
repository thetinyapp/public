The app

KcalOS is a straightforward calorie and macro tracker. You log what you ate against a built-in food table — or type a number straight in — and it shows where the day stands and how the week went. It needs an account, and your diary is saved to it; the app keeps its own copy on your phone so it opens and works with no connection. If you ask, it can also estimate a meal from a photo or a description.

Accuracy

KcalOS calculates from reference data, from what you enter, and — when you ask for one — from an AI estimate of a photo or a description. Reference values are approximations, what you enter may be estimated, and an AI estimate is a guess from what it was shown, which you can correct before you save it. Your calorie and macro targets are not a guess: they come from a published equation and never from a model. Treat every number as a useful estimate rather than a measurement, and do not rely on KcalOS where precision matters medically.
