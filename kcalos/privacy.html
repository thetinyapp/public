Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

KcalOS is a calorie-tracking app. This policy explains what data KcalOS handles. The short version: everything stays on your device. KcalOS makes no network connections, has no server, contains no analytics or advertising, and sends nothing anywhere.

What KcalOS stores

KcalOS stores the meals you log, your body details and calorie target, your weight entries, and your app preferences. All of it is written to the app's own storage on your device.

KcalOS has no account and no sign-in. It never asks for your name, email or any identifier, because there is nothing to sign in to.

What we collect

Nothing. Not anonymised usage data, not crash reports, not diagnostics, not an advertising identifier. KcalOS contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no third-party libraries of any kind. It has no code capable of making a network request, so there is no path by which your data could reach us or anyone else — including us.

What we do not do

  • We do not receive, store or transmit anything you enter.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data — we never have it.
  • We do not track you, profile you, or serve advertising.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, crash reporting or SDKs.

Your data, your device

Because your data never leaves your device, you control it completely. Deleting the app deletes everything it holds. There is no copy on a server for us to delete, and no request you need to send us to have it removed.

If your device backs up to iCloud, your KcalOS data may be included in that backup. That backup is between you and Apple under Apple's terms; we have no access to it.

Children

KcalOS is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. If KcalOS ever gains a feature that sends data anywhere, this policy will say so plainly before that feature ships.
