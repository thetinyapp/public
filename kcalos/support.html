Support

Last updated: 18 August 2026

Need a hand with KcalOS? Email resources@thetinyapp.com and we'll get back to you. The answers below cover the most common questions.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, in the app's own storage. KcalOS has no server and makes no network connections at all, so nothing is uploaded or shared.

Is there an account?

No. KcalOS asks for nothing that identifies you, and there is no server to hold an account even if it did.

Where does the food data come from?

A food table bundled inside the app, derived from public reference data. It ships with the app rather than being fetched, which is why logging works with no connection.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app. All data lives in the app's own storage and goes with it.

Something is broken

Email resources@thetinyapp.com with your device model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened. KcalOS sends us no crash reports, so a description from you is genuinely the only way we learn about a problem.
