Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

STLD splits shared expenses between people. Unlike our other apps it is not device-only: a shared ledger has to reach everybody in the group, so there is a server and there is an account. This page says exactly what that server holds.

STLD is not released yet and has no users. This policy describes the app as it is being built, and will be dated again before the first release.

What is saved to your account

  • Who you are. Your name, email address and provider id from Google or Apple, whichever you signed in with, and your profile photo if you have one. There is no password, so there is none to store.
  • Your ledger. Groups, the people in them, expenses, payments, comments and activity.
  • Attachments — images and PDFs you add to an expense.
  • A location, but only on an expense you deliberately attached one to.
  • Push tokens for the devices you have signed in on, so notifications can reach them.
  • Counters of how many AI extractions you have used and what they cost to run.

What stays on your device

The whole ledger, so the app works with no signal at all. Also your search index, your queue of unsent changes, and local things the server has no use for, such as which prompts you have dismissed.

What we never collect

  • Your voice. When you speak an expense the audio is used to extract the fields and then discarded. Only the transcript and the fields it produced are kept.
  • Your contacts. The app never reads them. You add people by typing a name.
  • Your location, unless you attach one to an expense yourself.
  • Any bank or card details. STLD records who owes what. It never moves money and is never told how you moved it.

Who can see your ledger

Everything in a group is visible to every member of that group, including attachments. That is what a shared ledger is, and it is worth being explicit about: a receipt you add is a receipt your flatmates can open. Nothing is visible to anyone outside the group.

We do not sell anything to anyone, and there is no advertising network in the app.

Crash reports

When the app fails we record the kind of failure and where in the code it happened. We deliberately do not send the error's message, because a message can carry the thing that broke it — an amount, or somebody's name.

AI

When you photograph a receipt, speak an expense, or type one as a sentence, the content is sent to a third-party model provider to extract the fields.

It is not used to train models. That is the provider's default for this kind of traffic and we have not opted out of it.

It is not deleted the moment we are finished with it, though, and we would rather say so than imply otherwise. The provider keeps a copy for up to 30 days so it can investigate abuse, and longer if something is flagged. Retaining nothing at all is a separate agreement we do not currently have. If that changes, this page will change with it.

Deleting your account

From the app under Account, or by emailing resources@thetinyapp.com.

One thing we cannot delete. A placeholder carrying your display name stays in every group you were part of, and the expenses and comments you wrote stay attached to it. This is not an oversight. An expense you paid for is part of what other people owe each other, and removing it would quietly change their balances. Your identity goes; your share of the arithmetic stays.

Erased immediately: your account, your link to Google or Apple, your email address, phone, photo, settings, push tokens and AI counters. Your account stops working at once. Backup copies age out within 30 days.

Deletion is refused while you owe money or are owed money in any group. The app lists which ones, so you can settle up first. Settling is how a debt ends; deleting your account is not.

Where your data lives

On servers in India (Supabase, ap-south-1), with attachments on Cloudflare R2.

Children

STLD is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is stored or who can see it, the app will say so rather than leaving it here to be discovered.
