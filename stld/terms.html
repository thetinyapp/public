The app

STLD keeps track of shared expenses between people and works out who owes whom. It is free. You sign in with Google or Apple so that a group can be shared across everybody's devices.

STLD does not move money

This is the most important line on this page. STLD is a record of what people owe each other. It is not a payment service, a bank, a wallet or a money transmitter, it holds no funds, and it never moves anything between anyone. Marking a debt as settled records that you say it was settled; it does not pay it. Whatever you actually use to pay each other is between you and has nothing to do with us.

STLD is not financial or legal advice

What the app shows you is arithmetic on figures you entered. It is not advice, it does not decide who owes what as a matter of law, and it settles no disputes. If a disagreement over money matters, resolve it between yourselves rather than by pointing at a screen.

Accuracy is shared

STLD is only as correct as what is typed into it, and in a group that means what everybody types into it. Any member can add, edit or delete anything in a group they belong to — that is deliberate, because a shared ledger that only one person can correct is a ledger that stays wrong. It also means somebody else can change a number you relied on. Check before you settle.

Where the app reads a receipt, hears a spoken expense, or converts a currency, it is estimating. Treat those as a starting point to be reviewed, which is why the app always shows you what it extracted before saving it.

Your group is visible to your group

Everything you put in a group — expenses, comments, attachments — is visible to every member of it. Do not put anything in a group that you would not want every member to read. See the Privacy Policy.

Adding other people

You can add people to a group by name before they have the app. When you do, you are recording your own view of a shared arrangement — please only add people who are actually part of it, and only invite people who want to be invited.
