Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

StrengthOS is a training-log app. This policy explains what data StrengthOS handles. The short version: what you log stays on your device. StrengthOS has an account so it can keep you signed in and let you delete that account; it does not upload your log, and it contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

What StrengthOS stores on your device

StrengthOS stores the workouts you log, your routines, your records and estimated maxes, and your app preferences. All of it is written to the app's own storage on your device, and none of it is uploaded. Signing in does not sync it; two people signing in on one device still share one log.

Your account

StrengthOS requires Sign in with Apple. When you sign in, Apple gives us two things: a random identifier that stands for you within our apps, and an email address — your real one or, if you choose Hide My Email, a private relay address Apple manages. We never see your Apple password.

On our server we store that identifier, that email address, and session records — which app you signed in to, an identifier for the device, and timestamps — so you stay signed in and can see and revoke your sessions. That is the complete list; your log is not on it.

What we do not do

  • We do not receive, store or transmit what you log in the app.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not track you, profile you, or serve advertising.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account from inside the app. Deletion is immediate: the account, its email and every session are removed from our server, and existing sign-ins stop working at once. What is stored on your device goes when you delete the app.

If your device backs up to iCloud, your StrengthOS data may be included in that backup. That backup is between you and Apple under Apple's terms; we have no access to it.

Children

StrengthOS is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. If StrengthOS ever gains a feature that sends your log anywhere, this policy will say so plainly before that feature ships.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a deletion request you would rather make by email? Write to resources@thetinyapp.com.
