The app

Calsense turns what you ate into calories, macronutrients and micronutrients, measured against reference intakes adjusted for your body, your goal, your diet and any health conditions you tell it about. It needs an account, and your diary is saved to it; the app keeps its own copy on your phone so it opens and works with no connection.

Talk to a qualified professional before changing your diet, training or medication, particularly if you have a health condition, are pregnant, or are recovering from injury or illness. If you think you have a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services.

Accuracy

Calsense calculates from reference data, from what you enter, and — for dishes it does not know — from an AI estimate of a description or a photo. Reference values are approximations, what you enter may be estimated, and an AI estimate is a guess from what it was shown, which you can correct. Treat every number as a useful estimate rather than a measurement, and do not rely on Calsense where precision matters medically.
