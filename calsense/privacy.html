Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

Calsense is a nutrition-tracking app. This policy explains what data Calsense handles. The short version: what you log stays on your device. Calsense has an account so it can keep you signed in; it does not upload your log, and it contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking. When you ask for a dish to be analysed, the description you typed is sent to make the estimate — that is the one thing that leaves your device at your request, and it is explained below.

What Calsense stores on your device

Calsense stores the meals you log, your body measurements, your goal and targets, any health conditions or dietary patterns you enter, your hydration and supplement records, and your app preferences. All of it is written to the app's own storage on your device, and none of it is uploaded. Signing in does not sync it; two people signing in on one device still share one log.

Your account

Calsense requires an account, created with Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google. Either way the provider gives us two things: an identifier that stands for you within our apps, and an email address — your real one or, with Apple's Hide My Email, a private relay address Apple manages. We never see your Apple or Google password. Accounts made with different providers are joined only when their verified email addresses match; otherwise they are separate accounts.

On our server we store that identifier, that email address, and session records — which app you signed in to, an identifier for the device, and timestamps — so you stay signed in and can see and revoke your sessions.

If you enter them, your basic profile — birth date, sex, height and weight — is saved to that account so our other health apps you sign in to can share it, and so you do not type it twice. Each of these fields is optional; one you have not entered is simply absent.

When you ask for a dish to be analysed, we also record that an analysis happened — which account, which app, and when — so we can limit how many analyses a day each account gets. That record says nothing about the food. That is the complete list; your log is not on it.

Food analysis

Calsense first looks a dish up in a reference list built into the app, on your device. Only when the dish is not in that list, and you ask for it to be analysed, is the description you typed sent to our server. The server passes it to Anthropic, the AI provider whose model produces the nutrition estimate, and returns the estimate to your phone. We do not store the description; the resulting meal entry is saved only on your device. Anthropic processes the description as a service provider under its commercial terms, which do not permit it to train models on this data. Do not put personal details in a dish description — it is meant for food.

What we do not do

  • We do not receive or store your food log. The one exception you control: a dish description you explicitly send for analysis is transmitted to make the estimate, and is not stored.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not track you, profile you, or serve advertising.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs.

Deleting your data

To delete your account, email resources@thetinyapp.com and we will remove the account, its email, its profile fields, its analysis records and every session from our server within 30 days; existing sign-ins stop working once that is done. What is stored on your device goes when you delete the app.

If your device backs up to iCloud, your Calsense data may be included in that backup. That backup is between you and Apple under Apple's terms; we have no access to it.

Children

Calsense is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. If Calsense ever gains a feature that sends your log anywhere, this policy will say so plainly before that feature ships.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a deletion request you would rather make by email? Write to resources@thetinyapp.com.
