Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

CarveOS is a health app. This policy explains what data CarveOS handles. The short version: what you log stays on your device. CarveOS has an account so it can keep you signed in; it does not upload your log, and it contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

What CarveOS stores on your device

CarveOS stores your app preferences, and — as features arrive — what you log in the app. All of it is written to the app's own storage on your device, and none of it is uploaded. Signing in does not sync it.

Your account

CarveOS requires Sign in with Apple. When you sign in, Apple gives us two things: a random identifier that stands for you within our apps, and an email address — your real one or, if you choose Hide My Email, a private relay address Apple manages. We never see your Apple password.

On our server we store that identifier, that email address, and session records — which app you signed in to, an identifier for the device, and timestamps — so you stay signed in and can see and revoke your sessions. That is the complete list; your log is not on it.

What we do not do

  • We do not receive, store or transmit what you log in the app.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not track you, profile you, or serve advertising.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs.

Deleting your data

To delete your account, email resources@thetinyapp.com and we will remove the account, its email and every session from our server within 30 days; existing sign-ins stop working once that is done. What is stored on your device goes when you delete the app.

If your device backs up to iCloud, your CarveOS data may be included in that backup. That backup is between you and Apple under Apple's terms; we have no access to it.

Children

CarveOS is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. If CarveOS ever gains a feature that sends your log anywhere, this policy will say so plainly before that feature ships.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a deletion request you would rather make by email? Write to resources@thetinyapp.com.
